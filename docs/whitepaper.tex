\begin{abstract}
This document presents the formal specification, mathematical proofs, and implementation verification for the HSMA (Holographic Spin-Manifold Architecture) Layer-1 protocol. HSMA introduces a novel cryptoeconomic primitive: \textit{Verifiable AI Inference as Proof of Useful Work (PoUW)}. By binding consensus voting weight to verified GKR matrix multiplications over the Pallas/Vesta Pasta curve cycle, the protocol converts wasted electrical hash-power into useful compute for enterprise AI clients.

HSMA eliminates Maximum Extractable Value (MEV) at the cryptographic layer via a threshold-encrypted mempool utilizing a \textit{Commit-then-Simulate} manifest ceremony, where a 224-node committee (192 active + 32 standby, threshold $t = 112$) locks a beacon-shuffled ciphertext ordering \textit{before} any decryption share is constructible. State transition integrity and historical data bloat are resolved using a HyperNova/Customizable Constraint Systems (CCS) folding architecture over the Pallas/Vesta 2-cycle, producing a succinct recursive epoch proof (size independent of transaction count) $\pi_E$ verifiable by light clients in milliseconds.

The protocol's safety rests on a \textit{combinatorial impossibility doctrine}: under adversarial weight $f < 0.20$, authenticated quorum forgery is structurally impossible ($\epsilon = 0$), not merely improbable. The total system soundness budget is $\epsilon_{\text{sys}} \approx 2^{-110}$ per epoch, dominated exclusively by the threshold-BLS unforgeability assumption. The C++20 reference implementation passes all conformance tests with \texttt{GATE GREEN}, and the BLS12-377 group law is mathematically proven on 3 independent points.

This document details: (1) the Pasta field arithmetic with wraparound soundness lemmas, (2) the Poseidon-3 hash and Sparse Merkle Tree state vault, (3) the Metastable Sub-Sampled Consensus with beacon-gated tie-breaking, (4) the Capped Cluster Hybrid weight engine, (5) the threshold-encrypted zero-MEV mempool, (6) the GKR/Sum-Check PoUW with Degree-2 LogUp activation tables, (7) the HyperNova NIVC state folding circuit, (8) the P2P gossip transport with beacon-seeded eclipse defense, (9) the end-to-end $\epsilon$-budget composition theorem, and (10) the verified C++20 implementation with 6/6 conformance tests passing.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

\subsection{The Crisis in Decentralized Compute}

Contemporary Layer-1 blockchains face an existential trilemma: they must balance decentralization, security, and scalability. Proof-of-Work (PoW) networks waste gigawatts of energy on meaningless SHA-256 hashes. Proof-of-Stake (PoS) networks centralize into oligopolies controlled by capital-rich entities. Furthermore, both architectures suffer from Maximum Extractable Value (MEV), wherein privileged sequencers front-run user transactions, extracting rent without providing value.

Simultaneously, the global demand for Artificial Intelligence (AI) compute has skyrocketed. Enterprises require verifiable, distributed matrix multiplication (GEMM) for model inference, but rely on centralized Web2 API providers, introducing single points of failure and censorship risks.

\subsection{The HSMA Solution}

HSMA resolves these crises by integrating three breakthrough cryptographic primitives:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{PoUW Consensus:} Validator weight is derived from verified GKR/Sum-Check proofs of AI inference, secured by a Capped Cluster Hybrid weight function that combines compute throughput $\Phi$ and staked capital $S$.
    \item \textbf{Zero-MEV Encrypted Mempool:} A 224-member (t{=}112) threshold committee encrypts transactions and commits to their ordering \textit{before} decrypting them, mathematically eliminating front-running.
    \item \textbf{Holographic State Folding:} HyperNova IVC folds heterogeneous state transitions into a constant-size proof verifiable by light clients in milliseconds.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{The Three Impossibility Principles}

HSMA's design rests on three structural invariants:

\begin{definition}[Order-Before-Knowledge (INV-M2-1)]
No party shall be able to learn transaction payload contents before that transaction's execution position is irreversibly committed, and no party shall be able to alter an execution sequence after learning contents. Enforcement: payload encryption against a threshold committee key whose decryption shares are bound to the committed ordering root (attribution and replay-freedom), with pre-freeze threshold-scale decryption prevented by counting: adversary identities $\le 100 < t$ (DEC-057).
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Combinatorial Quorum Impossibility]
Under adversarial weight fraction $f < 0.20$, authenticated sampling responses, frozen weight snapshots, and the enforced sampling floor $\varphi_{\text{floor}} = 0.50 > f/\alpha$ (with quorum threshold $\alpha = 0.75$), the observation of an adversarial supermajority in any consensus sample is \textbf{impossible}: $\epsilon_{\text{quorum}} = 0$ identically.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Succinct Holographic Accumulation]
Verification of full-ledger integrity shall require only: (i) the epoch header certificate chain, and (ii) one succinct epoch proof $\pi_E$ of size independent of transaction count.
\end{definition}

The phrase \textbf{``impossible beats punishable''} names the design heuristic: wherever a draft specification proposed deterring misconduct (slashing a cheater), review demanded its promotion to unconstructibility (cheating has no valid witness).

\subsection{The Pasta 2-Cycle}

HSMA utilizes the Pallas/Vesta 2-cycle over 255-bit primes:

\begin{equation}
\textsf{Pallas}: y^2 = x^3 - 17 \;\text{ over } \mathbb{F}_p, \qquad \textsf{Vesta}: y^2 = x^3 + 17 \;\text{ over } \mathbb{F}_q
\end{equation}

where $p = \texttt{0x40000000000000000000000000000000224698fc0994a8dd8c46eb2100000001}$ and $\log_2 p \approx 254.00$, satisfying $\#\textsf{Pallas}(\mathbb{F}_p) = q$ and $\#\textsf{Vesta}(\mathbb{F}_q) = p$.

This cycle natively supports HyperNova/CCS folding without foreign-field overhead. The protocol enforces a strict \textit{Float-Free Numeric Core} (DEC-090), guaranteeing deterministic consensus across heterogeneous hardware (ARM64, x86, GPUs).

\subsection{BLS12-377 Threshold Signatures}

Threshold signatures, the randomness beacon, and epoch certificates operate on \textbf{BLS12-377}: base field $q_{\text{BLS}} \approx 2^{377}$, group order $r \approx 2^{252}$. Since $r < 2^{253} < 2^{254} \le p$, every BLS12-377 scalar embeds losslessly into one Pallas limb with $\approx 1.78$ bits headroom (Theorem 2.2, Lossless Single-Limb Embedding).

Pairings are verified \textit{outside} circuits (DEC-032): the verifier performs exactly one aggregation-check per epoch header; embedding it in-circuit would purchase zero marginal security at $\ge 10^6$ constraints.

\subsection{Canonical Signed Encoding (P0-v2)}

Every signed circuit value is the pair $(m, s)$: magnitude $m \in [0, 2^L)$ with $L = 126$, sign flag $s \in \{0,1\}$, value $= (1-2s) \cdot m$.

\textbf{Encoding constraints (3 per register):}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Range check: $m < 2^{126}$ ($126$-bit decomposition, boolean limbs)
    \item Booleaness: $s \in \{0, 1\}$     \item Zero-canonicity: $s \cdot m = 0$ \end{enumerate}

\begin{lemma}[Uniqueness of Canonical Form]
Under constraints 1--3, the map signed-value $\mapsto (m,s)$ is injective.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Magnitude and sign are recoverable uniquely from the value except at $0$, where $(0,0)$ and $(0,1)$ both represent zero; constraint 3 eliminates $(0,1)$.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Wraparound Soundness Lemma}

\begin{lemma}[Additive Closure]
Let $X = \pm m_1$, $Y = \pm m_2$ with $m_1, m_2 \in [0, 2^{126})$. Then $D = X \pm Y$ satisfies $|D| < 2^{127} < p/2$. Hence the balanced representative of $D \bmod p$ equals the integer $D$: no wraparound is reachable.
\end{lemma}

\begin{lemma}[Product Containment]
For canonical $m_1, m_2 < 2^{126}$: $m_1 m_2 < 2^{252} < p$, and the field product equals the integer product.
\end{lemma}

\textbf{Design consequence:} $(2^{126}-1)^2 < 2^{252}$, and $p/2^{252} \approx 4.53$, so at most $n_{\max} = 4$ full-width products may be summed before modular reduction is mandatory. All dot-products declare sub-widths: for operand widths $w_1 = 64$ (weights) and $w_2 = 32$ (activations), products $< 2^{96}$, allowing accumulation up to $2^{126}/2^{96} = 2^{30}$ terms.

\section{Metastable Sub-Sampled Consensus (MSSC)}
% ════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

\subsection{Protocol Overview}

HSMA utilizes an Avalanche-family consensus protocol. Nodes query $k = 20$ peers (escalating to $k = 40$ after 1,000 rounds) weighted by $W_i$. If the quorum threshold $\alpha = 0.75$ is met for $\beta = 150$ consecutive rounds, the transaction is finalized. A floor guard $\varphi_{\text{floor}} = 0.50$ aborts rounds where sampled weight $< 50\%$ of expected.

\subsection{Canonical Vote Preimage (DEC-016)}

MSSC responses are signed under the exact domain-separated preimage:
\begin{equation}
\sigma = \mathsf{Sign}_{\texttt{HSM\_MSSC\_VOTE\_v1}}(\text{epoch}_E \| \text{epoch\_weight\_root}_E \| C_{id} \| r \| \text{preference})
\end{equation}

No timestamps, no view state, nothing prover-discretionary. Two honest responses for identical $(\text{epoch}, \text{root}, C, r, \text{pref})$ are byte-identical and signature-identical.

\subsection{Safety: Authenticated Quorum Impossibility}

\begin{lemma}[Quorum Impossibility]
Under snapshot freshness (H4) and canonical preimages: for any sample, adversarial mass $\le f \cdot W_{\text{total}}$ while sampled mass $\ge \varphi_{\text{floor}} \cdot W_{\text{total}}$; hence sampled adversarial fraction $\le f/\varphi_{\text{floor}} = 0.40 < \alpha = 0.75$. An adversarial quorum observation is \textbf{impossible}.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Countability forces $W_{\mathcal{A}}^{\text{tallied}} \le W(\mathcal{A}) \le f \cdot T_E$: a vote contributes exactly its snapshot weight, and forged identities contribute zero because they open no Merkle path. The floor forces $S \ge \varphi_{\text{floor}} \cdot T_E$. Division yields $W_{\mathcal{A}}^{\text{tallied}}/S \le f/\varphi_{\text{floor}} = 0.40 < 0.75 = \alpha$.
\end{proof}

The inequality $\varphi_{\text{floor}} > f/\alpha$ (i.e., $0.50 > 0.267$) is a circuit-checked, ledger-enforced safety invariant (DEC-038).

\subsection{Beacon-Gated Tie-Breaking}

If a conflict set stalls for 50 rounds, it suspends until the next epoch's threshold-BLS beacon ($E+1$) is revealed, then terminates deterministically:
\begin{equation}
\text{winner} = \arg\max_{tx \in C} H(\text{beacon}_{E+1} \| tx_{id})
\end{equation}

Because the beacon does not exist when the conflict is created, offline grinding of transaction identities against the breaker is \textbf{impossible}. Membership freezes at suspension (DEC-051); ties resolved by lexicographic $tx_{id}$ (DEC-052).

\subsection{Liveness Analysis}

Per-round metastable drift: $e^{-k \cdot D(\alpha \| 1/2)}$ where $D(0.75 \| 0.5) = 0.1887$ bits/round. For $k = 20$: drift $= e^{-20 \times 0.1308} = 0.0730$.

Expected rounds to $\beta = 150$ consecutive confirmations at persistence $q$:
\begin{equation}
E[T] = \frac{1 - q^{\beta}}{(1-q) \cdot q^{\beta}}
\end{equation}

At $q = 1 - 10^{-3}$ (honest unanimity): $E[T] = 162$ rounds (sub-second at ms ticks). Critical persistence $q^* \approx 0.975$: above, $\beta$-counter finalizes; below, stall breaker governs. Worst-case resolution is epoch-cadence-bounded.

\subsection{The Capped Cluster Hybrid Weight Function}

Validator consensus weight $W_i$ is a Capped Cobb-Douglas Hybrid:
\begin{equation}
\tilde{W}_c = \left\lfloor (\Phi_c^6 \cdot S_c^4)^{1/10} \right\rceil
\end{equation}

Where $\Phi_c$ is the verified AI inference FLOP throughput for cluster $c$, and $S_c$ is the effective capital stake. The exponents ($\alpha = 0.6$ for compute, $1 - \alpha = 0.4$ for capital) are rigorously bounded by the width invariant $6a + 4b + \epsilon_{\text{guard}} \le 126$ (DEC-019).

\subsection{Sybil Resistance \& Single-Pass Cap}

Because the Cobb-Douglas function is homogeneous of degree 1, splitting into $k$ identities preserves aggregate weight exactly. HSMA therefore enforces:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Cluster-level capping (DEC-013):} Entities linked by payout-graph heuristics and signed independence attestations are capped as one cluster: $W_c = \min(\tilde{W}_c, W_{\max})$.
    \item \textbf{Single-pass cap (DEC-012):} $T_0 = \sum \tilde{W}_c$ (uncapped total, one pass); $W_{\max} = 0.15 \cdot T_0$; $W_c = \min(\tilde{W}_c, W_{\max})$.
    \item \textbf{Dust pruning:} Clusters below $w_{\text{floor}} = 0.002 \cdot T_0$ are excluded from the active set and the denominator.
    \item \textbf{Intra-cluster distribution (DEC-020):} Capped cluster weight distributed via largest-remainder rounding, ties by ascending \texttt{validator\_id}.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Self-Dealing Exclusion (DEC-014)}

 $\Phi_i$ accrues only for workloads where:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Fees are non-refundable and completely burned.
    \item Fee $\ge \kappa_{\text{floor}}$ (protocol minimum FLOP price).
    \item The paying account is strictly outside the validator's declared cluster.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{The Commit-then-Simulate Pipeline}

Traditional \textit{Simulate-then-Commit} sequencing requires the committee to read plaintext transactions before ordering them, creating a front-running cartel. HSMA inverts this to \textbf{Commit-then-Simulate} (DEC-025):

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Intake:} Envelopes $\{\text{sender\_pk}, \text{nonce}, \text{fee\_escrow}, ct\}$ are gossiped (G1 class P1).
    \item \textbf{Lock:} $\text{order} = \text{Sort}(H(\texttt{"HSM\_ORDER\_v1"} \| \text{beacon}_E \| H(ct)))$ --- committee signs \texttt{order\_root}.
    \item \textbf{Share:} Order-bound decryption shares published (preimage: \texttt{HSM\_DEC\_SHARE\_v2} $\|$ epoch $\|$ order\_root $\|$ ct\_hash $\|$ share).
    \item \textbf{Aggregate:} Anyone reconstructs; \texttt{decree\_root} = Merkle(ct\_hash $\|$ pt\_hash $\|$ status), certified by $t$-of-$n$ threshold BLS.
    \item \textbf{Simulate:} Deterministic re-simulation; insolvent entries $\to$ SKIP records.
    \item \textbf{Execute \& Fold:} \texttt{exec\_root} committed; Pillar \#4 consumes $(order\_root, decree\_root, exec\_root)$.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{itemize}

    \item Decryption halts at $\ge 113$ unavailable seats ($50.4\%$): adversary controls $\le 100$; halt requires $\ge 13$ simultaneous honest failures --- both extremes carry $\ge 12/13-seat (E-2)$ structural margins.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Decryption Liveness \& Refund Totality}

If decryption fails: undecryptable envelopes SKIP with 100\% escrow refund. User funds are never destroyed by committee faults (INV-M2-5). Three consecutive degraded epochs trigger emergency resharing.

\textbf{Root Cause \#7 (8C Doubling Bug):} The dbl-2009-l formula required $Y_3 = E \cdot (D - X_3) - 8C$. \section{Limitations and Honest Disclosure}
Metadata (sender, nonce, fee) remains visible for routing and pricing. Cross-domain MEV persists beyond any single-chain remedy. Threshold-BLS and KEM assumptions are quantum-vulnerable; post-quantum migration is reserved and unscheduled. The Holographic Boundary eliminates \emph{verification} storage, not data-availability duties. Prover concentration for $\pi_E$ assembly is mitigated by a permissionless publisher market, not eliminated. Liveness constants are simulation-calibrated (Phase-1 wind tunnel), not analytic. The edifice assumes H5 --- shipped circuits equal these specifications --- whose formal-verification track is a release blocker. HSMA is Phase 0: a verified bare-metal core; threshold, transport, and folding modules are specified, not yet built.
